Our approach

At Infinite Financial Group, we believe financial confidence begins with understanding your options.

We help individuals, families, and business owners have meaningful conversations about their finances and take practical steps toward their goals.

Whether you want to protect your family with life insurance, organize your budget, address debt, or explore retirement options, we start by listening. Your priorities, responsibilities, and vision for the future guide the conversation.

Who we are

Veteran-owned. Faith-based. Built to serve.

Infinite Financial Group is a veteran-owned, faith-based financial services agency founded and led by Kareem M. Farley. Our approach reflects a commitment to service, integrity, education, and responsible stewardship.

Kareem brings his experience as a U.S. Army veteran, pastor, law enforcement professional, and business owner to his work with families. His commitment to serving others shapes our belief that people deserve clear explanations, respectful conversations, and support as they make important financial decisions.

Financial Protection

Life insurance & family protection

We help clients explore life insurance options based on their needs, priorities, and budgets.

  • Term life
  • Permanent life
  • Final expense
  • Mortgage protection

We also help you understand your existing coverage and review beneficiary designations so you can make more informed decisions.

Wealth Creation

Financial coaching & education

Coaching that helps you understand where your money goes and build a practical plan for moving forward.

  • Budgeting
  • Spending awareness
  • Debt organization
  • Savings goals
  • Financial habits

Through education and accountability, we help turn broad goals into manageable action steps.

Wealth Creation

Retirement education & insurance solutions

We help you discuss retirement goals, explore annuity options, and better understand retirement income and existing retirement accounts.

  • Retirement goals
  • Annuities
  • Retirement income
  • Rollover questions

We explain options clearly, including relevant costs, limitations, and tradeoffs. When a need falls outside our services, we encourage you to work with the appropriately qualified professional.

Legacy Planning

Legacy conversations

Legacy includes the values, knowledge, and resources you pass to the next generation. We help families begin those conversations.

  • Financial protection
  • Beneficiaries
  • Long-term goals
  • Financial education

Legal estate planning documents and tax matters should be addressed with qualified attorneys and tax professionals.
